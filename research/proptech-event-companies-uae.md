## 🏆 TIER 1 — Major Dubai / UAE PropTech Events (Attend + Speak)

### 1. PropTech Connect 2026 — Dubai
- **Organizer:** Dubai Land Department (DLD) + PropTech Connect Ltd
- **Status:** World's largest PropTech conference & exhibition
- **Location:** Dubai
- **Focus:** AI, blockchain, data-driven property solutions, real estate tech
- **Why attend:** DLD-backed credibility; global investors, developers, and tech innovators
- **Speaking opportunity:** Yes — pitch to DLD / PropTech Connect Ltd
- **Website:** [propertynewsint.com coverage](https://www.propertynewsint.com/en-ae/news/dubai-is-hosting-prop-tech-connect-2026-the-world-s-largest-real-estate-tech-event)
- **Contact angle:** Dubai Land Department Smart Real Estate Initiatives team

---

### 2. Future PropTech Summit Dubai 2026
- **Organizer:** Future PropTech Summit (independent)
- **Status:** 3rd edition — premier global PropTech event in the Middle East
- **Location:** Dubai
- **Focus:** PropTech founders, real estate leaders, AI innovators, smart city developers, policy makers, global investors
- **Why attend:** High-quality speaker roster; ideal for founder positioning
- **Speaking opportunity:** Yes — actively look for PropTech / AI real estate voices
- **Website:** [futureproptechsummit.com](https://futureproptechsummit.com/)
- **Key angle:** AI in real estate, next-gen infrastructure

---

### 3. International Property Show (IPS) 2026
- **Organizer:** IPS / supported by Dubai Land Department
- **Status:** One of the world's most important real estate exhibitions
- **Location:** Dubai
- **Focus:** Developers, brokers, investors, PropTech innovation
- **Why attend:** High developer + investor foot traffic; B2B deal-making
- **Speaking opportunity:** Yes — innovation & technology stages
- **Key angle:** Transparency, speed, customer engagement

---

### 4. Property Technology ConFex UAE
- **Organizer:** ConFex Events
- **Date:** April 30 – May 1, 2025 (annual)
- **Location:** Dubai
- **Format:** Highly targeted (invitation-only) — prequalified C-suite buyers
- **Focus:** CIOs, CTOs, COOs, Heads of Property Management, Asset Management
- **Why attend:** Not a trade show — closed peer group of decision-makers
- **Speaking opportunity:** Limited but high-impact; great for thought leadership credibility
- **Website:** [ctf-uae.com/proptech](https://ctf-uae.com/proptech)

## 🌍 TIER 2 — Global PropTech Events (Presence + Speaking)

### 5. Proptech Connect — London / Europe
- **Organizer:** Proptech Connect Ltd
- **Status:** Europe's largest PropTech event
- **Location:** London (also Dubai edition)
- **Focus:** Global PropTech ecosystem, investors, founders, operators
- **Website:** [proptechconnect.com](https://proptechconnect.com/)
- **Key angle:** Great for building global network; content shareable on LinkedIn

---

### 6. MIPIM — Cannes, France
- **Organizer:** Reed MIDEM
- **Status:** World's leading real estate market / conference
- **Location:** Cannes, France (March annually)
- **Focus:** International real estate deals, developers, investors
- **Why attend:** Network with global institutional investors and sovereign funds
- **Key angle:** UAE developers very active here

---

### 7. CityScape Global — Dubai
- **Organizer:** Informa Markets
- **Status:** MENA's largest real estate exhibition
- **Location:** Dubai World Trade Centre (October annually)
- **Focus:** Off-plan sales, developer launches, international buyers
- **Why attend:** Largest buyer audience in MENA; media coverage
- **Key angle:** Launch new projects; speaking panels available
