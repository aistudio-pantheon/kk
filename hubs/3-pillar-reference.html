How to Post — Instagram

  • Format: Reel, 45–90 seconds
  • Audio: Original voice-over ONLY. No trending audio.
  • Caption: 600+ chars · first person · story arc (setup → tension → resolution)
  • Hashtags: 0–2 max

Caption Formula

  • Last line: One honest sentence. No CTA. No question.
  • Start: "I" / "Tonight" / "There was a day…"
  • Never start with "Grateful to…" or "Excited to share…"
