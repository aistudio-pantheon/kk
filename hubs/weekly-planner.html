The 3 content types

❤️ Emotional40%

Human connection — personal stories, cultural moments, team & values, vulnerability.

Format: Reel 45–90s · original voice · 600+ char caption · 0–2 hashtags · no CTA · Tue 12–15h UTC.

🚀 Follower Growth35%

Authority & credibility — market intel, builder's diary, lessons, collabs. LinkedIn-primary.

Format: Reel 45–75s or carousel · 500–700 chars · numbered points · soft question CTA · 1–3 hashtags.

⚡ Controversy25%

Bold, defensible market takes. Attack ideas, never people. The debate is the distribution.

Format: Reel 30–60s · bold claim in 3s · 0 hashtags · end "Disagree? Tell me why." · max 1/week.
