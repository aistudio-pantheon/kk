✅ Production Rules — Do's & Don'ts
Non-negotiable for every piece of content on @kalpesh.kinariwala

❤️ Emotional — Always

  • Original voice-over only — zero trending audio
  • 600+ char caption, first person, story arc
  • Start: "I" / "Tonight" / specific sensory detail
  • Tuesday 12–15h UTC — non-negotiable
  • 0 hashtags (max 2 if truly necessary)
  • No CTA, no links, no project mentions
  • Film within 24h of HOP Events concert

🚀 Growth — Always

  • One clear insight per post — not a list of facts
  • Evidence from Pantheon/HOP pipeline — not news
  • LinkedIn is PRIMARY for Growth content
  • Numbered points in captions — saveable content
  • Soft question CTA: "What are you seeing?"
  • Site visits: hard hat + real active site, not showroom
  • Artist collabs: IG native Collab feature mandatory

⚡ Controversy — Always

  • Bold claim in first 3 seconds — no preamble
  • Steelman the opposing view before your evidence
  • Attack ideas — NEVER people or competitors
  • End every post: "Disagree? Tell me why."
  • 0 hashtags — shares are the distribution
  • Reply every comment within 2h — mandatory
  • Max 1 controversy post per week

🚫 ABSOLUTE RED LINES — All Content Types

NEVER on personal feed:Project launches · "Book Now" CTAs · Unit pricing · Payment plan offers · Award posts without personal story
NEVER boost until:Post has cleared 9,600 organic engagements (2× avg). Boost on Reach objective only. No exceptions.
NEVER start a caption:"Grateful to…" · "Excited to share…" · "Honored to announce…" · "Check out our new…"
